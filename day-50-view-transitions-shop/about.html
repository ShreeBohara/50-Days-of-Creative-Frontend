The shop

Fifty years of
good noise

Wavelength opened in 1974 with a single crate of soul 45s and a landlord who accepted payment in Coltrane bootlegs. The crate multiplied. The landlord retired to Marrakesh. We stayed.

The rules of the house have not changed: listen before you buy, the booth in the back is first come first served, and if you ask for the good stuff we will ask what you had for breakfast, because the answer matters.

This little site is also a demo. Every page you visit here is a real HTML page — no framework, no client-side router, not a single line of JavaScript. The morphing you see as you move between pages is the browser’s native cross-document View Transitions API, switched on with a few lines of CSS. Where the API is not supported, the shop quietly falls back to what it always was: a fast, plain, multi-page website.
